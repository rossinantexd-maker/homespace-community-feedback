# Fishing — community mechanics wishlist

Everything the community has proposed about the **fishing** feature, gathered from two places:

- **Bilibili** — the video *"中国玩家想要钓鱼，我们就做了钓鱼🎣"* ([BV19NTc6VEfg](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV19NTc6VEfg/)), where players were explicitly asked how they picture the fishing mechanic. 306 comments; ~103 top-level read.
- **QQ groups** — official Homespace community groups (2群 / 3群 / 489209963), messages up to 2026-07-07.

Compiled 2026-07-07. Source is tagged on each point: `[B]` = Bilibili, `[QQ]` = QQ. Chinese fishing terms are kept in brackets where they carry meaning.

> **The single loudest signal:** almost everyone benchmarks against **Russian Fishing 4 (俄罗斯钓鱼4 / 俄钓4)** — they want its *physical feel* but explicitly **without** its punishing hardcore design (endless no-bite sessions, brutal rare-fish grind). "Beat 俄钓4" is repeated dozens of times. `[B][QQ]`

---

## A. Core catching feel

- **Match RF4's feel, drop the hardcore.** RF4 is named the current market leader for simulation feel, but hated for too few bites and hours with nothing. Aim for that feel, easier. `[B]`
- **Casting & fighting loop (most detailed proposal):** left mouse to cast; charge/hold time = cast distance; farther from shore = deeper water = faster bite; bobber sinking = bite; move mouse left/right to tire and land the fish (溜鱼). `[B]`
- **Fight big fish physically.** Hooking a giant should be a pull/tug struggle; if the character's stats are too low, the fish drags them into the water. `[B]`
- **Rod elasticity.** Feedback on the current build: the rod is stiff — real rods flex. Add rod flex/elasticity. The bite → reel → out-of-water steps aren't in yet. `[QQ]`
- **Rod & tackle choice:** soft rod vs hard rod; allow rod snapping (爆竿, "every angler has snapped one"). `[B]`
- **Controller support + haptic vibration** on the strike/catch. `[B]`
- **Skill tree is contested — keep progression OPTIONAL and light.** One camp wants a tree (faster bite, auto-lift small fish, fish harder to shake off, highlighted/enlarged bobber, shorter fight time, easier at higher level). Another camp says **no skill tree** — trees deter new players and limit fun, exactly RF4's over-restriction. `[B]`

## B. Skunking / "air force" (空军) culture

- The 空军 meme (going home with nothing) is a core part of Chinese fishing culture — players *want* a realistic chance of catching nothing, but **not frustratingly high**. Make the skunk rate tunable; let skill matter ("bad technique → get skunked hard"). `[B][QQ]`
- At minimum, give a splash/near-miss so a skunked player still sees something. `[B]`

## C. Chinese-style fishing (localize the fantasy)

- **Prefer hand-rod / traditional & platform fishing (手竿 / 传统钓 / 台钓) over lure fishing (路亚)** — 路亚 arrived in China later; 台钓 is both more familiar *and* easier to implement. `[B]`
- **台钓 depth players want:** float tuning (调漂 — "一年开饵三年调漂"), line-rig matching (线组搭配), hook-type selection (钩型选择), bait flavor/scent selection (饵料味型/香型), fishing-depth setting (钓棚), bottom vs float fishing (钓底/钓浮). `[B]`
- **Chumming (打窝)** before casting; casting frequency (打频率). `[B][QQ]`
- Match line / float / hook so the target fish can take the bait cleanly. `[B]`

## D. Environment & simulation depth

- **Multiple maps/regions**, each country/region with its own signature "legendary" fish — make them look beautiful. `[B]`
- **Boats → sea fishing (海钓).** `[B]`
- **Time of day** changes which fish appear (day/night fishing). `[B]`
- **Weather + four seasons** affect fishing (and farming): ice fishing / rain fishing / night fishing bonuses; different hydrology/geography → different fish. Multiple QQ users ask directly "will weather affect fishing & farming?" `[B][QQ]`
- **Remove the water/land "air wall"** other fishing games use (where you can only transition by boat) — allow natural shore interaction. `[B]`
- **Living ecosystem:** fish schooling (a shoal swimming together looks great); predator–prey behavior (carnivores chase omnivores, big fish chase small). `[B]`

## E. The fish themselves

- **Fish catalog / picture-book (图鉴)** like Animal Crossing (动森) / Zelda: each fish with size, weight, encyclopedia info. `[B]`
- **Size/weight variation within a species**, reflected in the model (heavier = bigger, or S/M/L tiers). `[B]`
- **Rare variants at low probability:** mutations, giant size, albino, colored scales (à la theHunter: Call of the Wild). `[B]`
- **Species variety:** ornamental fish, tropical fish, pufferfish; distinguish ornamental vs edible fish. `[B]`
- **Baits & tackle system:** craftable baits, feed types, dedicated bait for a specific target fish; rod tiers; different tackle catches different fish. `[B]`
- **Fish farming / breeding (养殖)**; close the loop **catch → raise → eat** as one connected system ("Chinese players: fish, raise fish, eat fish — link it all and it stays playable"). `[B][QQ]`
- (co-op flavor) **poisonous fish** that knock you down when landed — funny in multiplayer. Mermaids (joke). `[B][QQ]`

## F. Aquarium & display — biggest recurring theme (ties fishing to the house, the game's core)

- **Keep caught fish in a tank AND a pond** (养在鱼缸和池塘); tanks as live-creature objects. `[B][QQ]`
- **Aquascaping:** water plants, shells, driftwood, shipwreck & treasure décor, tank lighting, wave-maker/pump; also shrimp and turtles. Reference **Behind Glass** for custom aquariums. `[B]`
- **Fish tank as architecture (strongly upvoted, ties straight into building):**
  - tank that becomes / embeds into a **wall** (in-wall tank; players already wedge tanks into walls to fake this); `[B][QQ]`
  - **glass floor** with fish swimming below + lighting; `[B][QQ]`
  - **glass pillars** with fish inside; `[B][QQ]`
  - **transparent glass ceiling** with fish overhead. `[B][QQ]`
- **Trophy display:** mount catches as trophies in the house — specimen/taxidermy wall (标本墙), or hang the fish to show off (like 异环 lets you hang a fish off your car). Named as a big playtime driver. `[B][QQ]`
- **Lake-view house:** fish directly from your bed / balcony ("lake-view room, bed on the roof, fish straight from bed"). `[QQ]`

## G. Reward design (from the sharpest critique — an RF4 postmortem)

- A game's core is to **create and sustain pleasure** and satisfy needs unmet in real life; an "impossible, self-labeled hardcore" difficulty is just bad design. `[B]`
- **RF4 flaw 1 — server-wide trophy drops:** trophies spawn for all online players at once, so some get repeats and others never get one. Better: **guarantee a trophy once a player has caught enough** — effort should always pay off. `[B]`
- **RF4 flaw 2 — session decay ("小时口"):** catch rate drops the longer you play, as if the fish "clock off." Fish are NPCs / a boss loot table — don't make them go off-shift. Keep the loop rewarding. `[B]`

## H. Scope cautions (meta-feedback — take seriously)

- **Don't over-add ("既要又要").** Strong, repeated worry that chasing every suggestion yields many shallow, rough systems. Nail the core (building) and polish fishing into ONE solid pillar; ship extra ideas later. Several fear the game "loses direction" if it over-listens. `[B][QQ]`
- **Push non-core content to Steam Workshop / mods (创意工坊)** — let the community grow content itself. Matches Artem's own stated plan: fishing is added as a NEW hook to attract players; extras and fish variety go to the Workshop. `[B][QQ]`
- Open questions players keep asking: is fishing weather-dependent? single-player or multiplayer/co-op? Add a **hunger/satiety system** so cooked fish is eaten because you're hungry (not just for stamina or trade). `[B]`
- Some frame fishing (and farming) as **DLC** rather than base scope. `[B]`
